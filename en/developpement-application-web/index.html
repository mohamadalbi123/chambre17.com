Web app development in La Rochelle

A web app lets your customers or team complete tasks online. Based in Lagord, CHAMBRE 17 helps businesses in La Rochelle that need bookings, a client portal, a dashboard or a tool built around the way they work.

Start with the tasks you want to simplify

The project starts with how your business works: who will use the tool, what information they need and which steps currently take time. Together, we identify the needs the application should address.

A concrete description is often the best starting point. For example, a customer might need to book a service or view information in their account, while your team might need a dedicated interface for managing products.

Choose features that support each journey

We define the structure and user experience before building the solution. Every feature should serve an identified use; the precise scope depends on your project.

  • Booking journeys to organise a request or a service.
  • A portal and customer accounts to provide a dedicated space.
  • A dashboard or internal tool for viewing and managing information.
  • E-commerce features, including products, customer accounts and checkout.

Review the application with its users in mind

During the review, you try the solution and tell us what needs adjusting. We revisit the planned journeys to check that they fit your business, then refine the details before launch.

The experience is tested and optimised across devices. For a tool used both at a desk and on the move, this helps us assess how clearly information is presented and how easily users can complete the main actions.

Plan for changes after launch

New needs can emerge through use: another piece of information, an adjustment to a journey or an additional feature. Optional monthly management can support those changes, technical maintenance and performance monitoring.

To discuss your application, describe who will use it, the tasks they need to complete and your current tools. This helps us understand the scope and prepare a proposal around the way your business works.
