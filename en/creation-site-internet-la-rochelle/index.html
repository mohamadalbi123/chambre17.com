Website design in La Rochelle

Your website should help customers understand your business and take the next step: contact you, explore your services or make a booking. CHAMBRE 17, a business registered in Lagord, helps professionals in La Rochelle create websites around their needs.

A website built around your business

We start by understanding your customers, your services and the questions people ask before getting in touch. This helps us define the pages you need, their content and the actions to highlight.

A shop, an independent professional and a service business may have different priorities: explaining an offer, presenting completed work or making appointments easier to book. Those goals guide the structure and design.

Clear journeys from first visit to contact

We design and build the website with phone and desktop use in mind. We choose the features with you according to the needs of your business.

  • Pages that explain your services and introduce your business.
  • Contact journeys or booking integration, depending on the project.
  • Multilingual content when it is relevant to your customers.

Reviewing the website together before launch

You explore the website, try the journeys and share your feedback. We refine the details together before launch, then test and optimise the experience across devices.

To prepare for our first conversation, you can gather your logo, a description of your services, some images and the contact details customers should find. An existing website can also help explain what you would like to improve.

Preparing for launch and what comes next

Digital launch support can complement your website with Google Business Profile setup, WhatsApp Business setup or basic conversion tracking. Together, we identify the elements that are useful for your project.

After launch, optional monthly management can keep content current, add pages and monitor performance. Your website can continue to evolve as your business changes.
