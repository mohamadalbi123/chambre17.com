Website maintenance and management in La Rochelle

Your business keeps changing after launch: new services, information to correct and pages to expand. CHAMBRE 17, a business registered in Lagord, offers optional monthly management for the websites and web apps of businesses in La Rochelle.

Keep your content useful and current

A changing service or a new offer should have a place on your website. Content management keeps the information customers read up to date and helps your website reflect your business.

Depending on the agreed needs, updates can cover text, existing content or new pages. The aim is to keep the journey clear as your range of services grows.

Combine technical maintenance and performance monitoring

Managing a website also involves its technical operation. CHAMBRE 17 can handle updates, maintenance and performance monitoring as part of the support agreed with you.

The needs depend on the website and its features. A business website, a booking journey and an application with customer accounts involve different uses. We start with your solution to define the work that will be useful.

Let your website evolve with your business

Support can also include ongoing improvements after launch. Your feedback and changes in your business help identify what to develop next.

  • Update content and the presentation of your services.
  • Add a page for a new offer or a new need.
  • Improve an existing feature or journey.
  • Monitor performance and identify possible improvements.

Define the support your website needs

Monthly management is optional after your website is built. We discuss the content to maintain, the changes you have in mind and the technical aspects to define the scope of support.

To prepare for this conversation, share your website address and describe the changes you want to make. If a journey is causing problems, identify the page and explain what you expected to happen. These details give us a concrete starting point and help distinguish routine updates from larger changes.
